WHO WE ARE

Learning that puts animal wellbeing first.

Pawsitive Pet Academy is a pet-care education and practical animal-training concept founded by Sarah Smith in 2023 and based in Durban.

Our proposed digital platform helps pet owners, families, pet sitters, kennel staff, veterinary assistants, aspiring entrepreneurs and animal lovers explore learning opportunities.
